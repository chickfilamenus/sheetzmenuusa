    Sheetz menu guide 2026: what to order and what it costs

    Sheetz runs a full kitchen inside a convenience store, so its menu feels closer to a diner than a gas station. Here's how to read the Sheetz menu, when to order what and how to keep the bill down.

    What makes the Sheetz menu different

    Three things set the Sheetz menu apart. First, almost everything is made to order (MTO): you build your food on a touchscreen instead of picking from a heated shelf. Second, most stores keep the kitchen running 24 hours a day, so a burger at 3 a.m. is normal. Third, the names are playful: the Shmuffin and Shmagel are breakfast sandwiches, Fryz are fries, and Shweetz covers the cookies and brownies.

    The Sheetz menu by time of day

    • Morning Bacon, Egg & Cheese Burrito about $3.19, Shmuffin $3.99, medium coffee $1.99.
    • Lunch 6" Meatball or Turkey Sub $4.99–$5.49, MTO chicken sandwiches $4.99, salads from $8.39.
    • Snack Mac 'n Cheese Bites $2.99, a $0.99 cookie, or a lemonade from $3.49.
    • Late night Classic Burger $3.99, Shnack Wrap $2.49, shareable Fryz and Mozzarella Sticks.

    Best-value picks under $5

    You can eat well at Sheetz without spending much. These items cost under $5 each at typical prices:

    • Customizable Coffee (M 16 oz) – $1.99
    • Sheetz Shnack Wrap – $2.49
    • Mac 'n Cheese Bites (5 pc) – $2.99
    • Bacon, Egg & Cheese Burrito – $3.19
    • Classic Burger (single) – $3.99
    • Meatball Sub (6") or any MTO chicken sandwich – $4.99

    Pair a Classic Burger, Mac 'n Cheese Bites and a medium coffee and the meal comes to $8.97 before tax.

    Ordering for a group

    Feeding a car full of people? The Bucket Feast (about $24.78) and My Tailgate for 2 – Chicken Combo (about $17.25) are built for sharing. Large sides such as Mozzarella Sticks ($9.39), Jalapeño Fryz ($9.99) and Wisconsin Cheese Bites ($11.39) also split easily between three or four people. You'll find every Fryz, bites and app on the Sheetz Sides & Appetizers Menu. Add everything to the kiosk calculator above and set "Split between" to see what each person owes.

    Drinks and desserts

    Coffee is the bargain: a medium is about $1.99, while lattes run $3.49–$4.29. For something cold, lemonades start at $3.49 and Refresherz are about $4.59. The cheezecake milkshakes ($5.49–$5.79) and the Firewerkz Shake ($6.29) are the dessert stars. Every flavour and size is on the Sheetz Milkshakes Menu. Smoothies cost $4.59–$6.99, and Shweetz treats start at $0.99 for a cookie.

    How to order from the Sheetz menu

    1. Tap the touchscreen in store, or open the Sheetz app to order ahead.
    2. Choose a category: burgers, subs, chicken, salads, breakfast, sides or drinks.
    3. Customise the bread, protein, cheese, toppings and sauce.
    4. Pay at the register with your receipt, or in the app.
    5. Pick up your food when your order number is called.

    Healthier choices on the menu

    If you're watching what you eat, look at the Low Calorie MTO Salad (about $8.39), the Plant-Based MTO Burger (about $6.49), the MTgo! Fresh Fruit Mix ($4.59) and grilled rather than crispy chicken. Because MTO lets you remove sauces and cheese, you control a lot of the calories. For allergen and nutrition details, check Sheetz's official nutrition information before ordering.

    Things to know about Sheetz prices

    Sheetz prices are not the same in every state or store. The prices here are typical prices and items marked "New" are approximate. Join Sheetz Rewardz to collect points, watch the app for weekly deals, and remember that delivery apps usually charge more than ordering in store.

    Sheetz menu questions

    Is Sheetz food made fresh?

    Yes. Most of the Sheetz menu is made to order (MTO), so your burger, sub, wrap or salad is prepared after you place the order on the touchscreen or in the app.

    How do I order from the Sheetz menu?

    Tap the MTO touchscreen in the store or use the Sheetz app. Choose your item, customise it, then pay at the register or in the app and collect it when your number is called.

    What can I get at Sheetz for under $5?

    At typical prices you can get a medium coffee ($1.99), a Shnack Wrap ($2.49), Mac 'n Cheese Bites ($2.99), a Bacon, Egg & Cheese Burrito ($3.19), a Classic Burger ($3.99) or a 6-inch Meatball Sub ($4.99).

    How much is a burger at Sheetz?

    A single Classic Burger is about $3.99. Newer burgers such as the Hot Spicy Chicken Burger are around $5.99 and the Plant-Based MTO Burger around $6.49, depending on your store.

    Does the Sheetz menu have healthy options?

    Yes. The Low Calorie MTO Salad, grilled chicken, the Plant-Based MTO Burger, the MTgo! Fresh Fruit Mix and smoothies are lighter picks, and MTO lets you skip sauces and cheese.

    What are Sheetz Fryz?

    Fryz is how Sheetz spells its fries. They come in several styles and in larger shareable orders such as the Jalapeño Fryz, which cost about $9.99.

    Can I order Sheetz online?

    Yes. You can order ahead in the Sheetz app for pickup, and many stores are also on delivery apps. Delivery prices and fees are usually higher than in-store prices.

    Is the Sheetz menu the same in every state?

    The core MTO menu is much the same, but prices and some limited-time items change by state and store. Check the Sheetz app for your local menu.
